Background · 0:30–2:00 · Speaks: Taz

PRA already sees the packs. Q&A is the gap.

What is already clear

Structured returns: total income, operating costs, credit impairment / ECL, CET1. Those four lines stay locked.

What is hard to use

Earnings-call Q&A is public, hedged, speaker-messy, and not in the supervisory scan every quarter.

Business question
Does analyst Q&A carry a leading signal those four reported lines miss — a temporal shift, a peer divergence, or a split between pack direction and narrative tone?

Our bet: yes when theme tone and reported direction diverge. When they agree but tone is soft, that is WATCH, not noise.

Public research frame: earnings language as a complement to regulatory returns, not a substitute. Supervisors need evidence IDs, not a black-box score.

Close · 12:45–13:45 · integration · Speaks: Taz

Workflow fit — and what will block it

How it sits on a desk

Overnight or quarterly job when IR files land. Output is a versioned pack: verdict, n, quotes, four-metric agree column, PRA one-pager. Human accepts or rejects the verdict.

Obstacles

Hedged language → transformer over-neutral. Small N / flat peers break gap rules. Labels are scarce — do not automate promotion. Change management: trust rules plus evidence, not model mystique.

Ethical / sustainable
We do not conclude either firm’s prudential condition. Alert / watch / null is a method on public text versus public packs. Confidential returns stay out of scope.
