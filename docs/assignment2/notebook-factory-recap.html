Stage-by-stage

1 Evidence — Susana · 3,218 turns · 1,002 pairs

pdfplumber extract, bank-specific speaker regex, analyst-only modelling corpus. Manifest 251 rows (137 PDFs + 114 packs). Extra years 2006–2026 are Hunter-prompted versus the A1 plan of 20 transcripts — disclose, not unnoticed scope creep.

Disclose, not a pre-pitch fix: 91 empty answers after Barclays bleed-split; 71 pairs with blank inherited date (119/137 transcripts dated). LCR/NSFR stay out of the four-line seed.

2 Topics — Debanjan (Alfred maps 8-way)

Deterministic four-bucket seed is the M4 fallback if clusters are unstable. BERTopic names come from reading Q&A, not top-words. LDA is an independent sanity check. Never treat residual −1 as a theme.

3 Sentiment + behaviour + 8-way — Alfred, Bupathi, Aidan

FinBERT scores question and answer separately. LDSA is the lexicon cross-check. M6 scores directness, metric coverage and substitution; high substitution is a WATCH input, not a FinBERT class called Avoidance. Optional Stage 3.1c Gemini vs FinBERT on 20 rows was 35% — skip without a key; do not recode gold.

4–5 Briefs and refinement — Rafael, Debanjan

DistilBART prompt-echo is not evidence. Rafael’s extractive briefs on income, costs, ECL and CET1 are the A2 quote surface.

6 Three views — Susana · calendar_period() wired

Temporal shift, matched peer (HSBC interim ↔ Barclays q2 as H1 via scripts/periods.py), and structured vs Q&A on the four lines. Missing bank → no gap (never zero). If either side is 100% FinBERT-neutral, peer ALERT cannot fire. 2025-H1 gap −0.126 is that case.

7–9 Status, protocol, publish

Stage 8 packs HSBC H1 as WATCH (A3). Stage 9 writes the PRA one-pager and the product subset the demo reads. Re-run 45–90 minutes. Stage 0 stubs unused ParametricUMAP so a protobuf mismatch cannot kill a fresh kernel.

10.2 Yahoo press — filtered, not a signal · 0 of 20 kept

yfinance ticker news is mentions, not own-results coverage. Stage 10.2 keeps a headline only if the bank is the subject and the title has earnings / results / profit / quarter / interim. Live fetch: 20 headlines, 0 kept (Netflix downgrades, TSEM upside, M&T earnings). Do not quote the unfiltered 0.04 gap. Same posture as the synthetic desk log: built, tried, honestly caveated.
